# Saltwind

Saltwind is a third-person tropical island exploration project for Unreal Engine 5.8.

## Open the project

Install Unreal Engine 5.8, Git LFS, and the Windows C++ build tools required by Unreal. After cloning, run `git lfs pull` and open `Saltwind.uproject`. Let Unreal build the game module when prompted. The startup map is Coral Reach.

## Explore

WASD moves the character and the mouse controls the camera. Hold Shift to sprint on dry ground. Space jumps on land or surfaces in water. Hold C to dive. R returns to the beach, and F1 toggles the controls.

Coral Reach cycles through morning, noon, afternoon, evening, night, and midnight over 32 minutes. Press N to blend to the next lighting phase over 20 seconds.

Press P to start or stop a preview shower. The ground dries gradually after rain.

## Collect and craft

Press E near wood or stone to collect it. Tab opens the inventory. Drag an item stack into the scene to drop it, or hold Shift while dragging to drop one piece.

Press Q to open crafting. Place any two pieces of wood and any two stones into the four ingredient slots. Drag the resulting campfire into your inventory or onto clear ground to light it. Placement is final. A campfire burns for up to 10 minutes, and rain extinguishes it sooner.
