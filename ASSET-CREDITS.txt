Unreal Engine Third Person template.

Treading animation from Universal Animation Library by Quaternius, CC0.
Retargeted to Manny.
https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html

Surface crawl and underwater breaststroke from Carnegie Mellon University
Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database subject 126, trials 11 and 04.
The database is free for all uses and was supported by NSF Grant #0196217.
The strokes were retargeted to Manny and edited into in-place loops.
Hip and ankle motion was adjusted for swimming without support.
Saltwind's authored wade-to-swim transition uses Manny's land walk
and the retargeted surface crawl as its starting and ending poses.
https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/
https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/search.php?subjectnumber=126
